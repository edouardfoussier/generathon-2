Historical dates describe intended scenes, not verified reconstructions.

Using G01 at about 72 in 2026 suggests birth around 1954, with memories at approximately 16 in 1970, 23 in 1977 and 41 in 1995. Earlier story documents used a 1955 birth; settle the exact script chronology when the team locks the story.
