## Bounded comparison batch

**18 subjects × 3 image models = 54 images:** 10 character sheets, 5 location sheets, 3 footwear sheets. Use the same identity references, composition, costume instructions and style brief for all three models. Each model produces one complete sheet per subject, not three unrelated castings. Choose one approved master per subject after comparison; do not alternate model outputs randomly in the final film.

The current script is `creative/converse-cinematic-script-v3.md`: attic → basketball → dance → wedding → newborn daughter → teenage daughter receives shoes → attic. No funeral, cemetery, present-day skate court or additional grandson is required. G01 and D06 and Luna's existing identity are explicit team choices; supporting cast and age progressions remain candidates.

Chronology: Rafa 16 in 1970, 23 in 1977, approximately 24 at the 1978 wedding, 25 at his daughter's 1979 birth, 41 in 1995 and 72 in 2026. Elena is approximately one year younger. Their daughter is newborn in 1979, 16 in 1995 and 47 in 2026. Luna is 15 in 2026. The older grandparents are included because the user requested references for the characters, even though neither has a present-day appearance in v3.

## Audit findings to carry into prompts

- `creative/selection/creative-choices.json` predates the three completed films: its `finalVideoGenerated: false` and v2 pointer are stale. The v3 script and recent model-comparison artifacts are the current story evidence.
- Original G01 generation text described a French-Moroccan fictional man. The film's later story calls the same selected face Rafa Morales, Puerto Rican. Preserve the chosen face; avoid changing it in response to a nationality keyword.
- Older family document says Elena wore other shoes; v3 explicitly changed these to red Converse. Follow v3.
- Shared wedding anchor visually obscures the black shoe collars enough to read as low-tops. Preserve face/costume, correct footwear from the product master.
- Shared gift anchor contains a broad cream side patch and a tan toe/rubber blob. These are inconsistent exploratory details, not a locked product design.
- Existing P01 also contains a tiny blue ink dash. It should not become an extra story symbol unless the team intentionally chooses it.
- Existing family-room plate R06 includes a small floor cord; omit it. Existing alternate attic R01 has an overturned stool; D06 is the geometric master.

Review the three model outputs side by side for face proportions, apparent age, hair, outfit, hands/feet, shoe collar height, badge side, patch size and room landmarks. A beautiful render does not by itself establish continuity.
