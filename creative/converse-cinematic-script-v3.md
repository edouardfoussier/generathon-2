# CONVERSE — Conserve what matters

Script v3 · Quick polish of the team's new direction · 26 September 2026

**76-second working cut, including a four-second endline.** The team approved this revision for three full model trials: Seedance 2.5, Kling 3 Pro and Veo 3.1. Production logs and edits are in [`model-comparison-v3`](model-comparison-v3/README.md). The earlier animatic remains available and follows v2.

## Editorial intention

Luna begins by questioning why her mother keeps old belongings. She ends by asking to hear the story behind them. The shoes turn inherited objects into a reason for a real conversation. Keep the AI exchange brief and matter-of-fact; the emotional payoff belongs to the family.

Résumé FR : Luna passe de l'agacement devant les affaires conservées à l'envie de connaître son grand-père. La paire traverse sa jeunesse, la rencontre avec Elena, le mariage, la naissance de leur fille et la transmission à cette fille — la mère de Luna. Au retour, Luna demande à sa mère de lui raconter son histoire.

## Continuity and production notes

- One inherited black adult pair runs through the entire story. The red pair belongs to Elena and remains a secondary visual accent. Her previous violet-dress casting can stay; footwear is a new wardrobe revision.
- Suggested chronology: Rafa16 in1970; Rafa23 and Elena22 meet in1977; wedding around1978; daughter born around1979; daughter16 in1995; daughter47 and Luna15 in2026. Dates need not appear on screen beyond the opening photograph.
- The 1979 baby and the1995 teenage daughter are the same person: Luna's mother. Do not insert an additional son/grandson branch.
- Use a jump shot without naming a point value. Frame the release, basket and reaction separately rather than requiring one long generated sporting action.
- Introduce the cream repair only in later memories. Hold the shoe's construction, initials and badge placement constant. Initials are inside; ankle badges belong on the inner sides.
- The opening does not require a funeral or recent death to explain the box. Avoid adding either without an intentional story decision.
- The AI screen is a short narrative beat. Build the interface and readable text in the edit; do not rely on generated screen lettering.
- Mom speaks natural English with a specific Puerto Rican influence. Cast a voice, rather than spelling an accent phonetically or exaggerating delivery.
- Use the Converse wordmark as a graphic asset in final post-production, with the corrected endline **“Conserve what matters.”** Do not ask the video model to invent a brand font or logo.
- If the cut feels crowded, compress wedding and newborn to2–3seconds each and return that time to the shoe handover and final exchange. Preserve the four-second endline.

## What changes from the current animatic

Reusable starting points: D06 attic, Luna's selected face and original wardrobe, black shoe references, young Rafa/court, Rafa/Elena faces and dance room, the1995 father-daughter care scene, adult Mom's matching reaction.

New or revised coverage: attic entrance and dialogue, discovery in a shoebox, phone insert, shoe-donning fragments, basketball release/net, Elena's red Converse, wedding, newborn, explicit shoe gift, and the final request to Mom. These require new images or motion; the existing v2 animatic does not demonstrate them.

Three simple cinema terms used above:

- **Insert:** a short close-up of an important object or detail.
- **Match cut:** two shots joined through a similar shape, framing or movement.
- **Sound bridge:** a sound starts before a cut or continues after it, linking the scenes.
