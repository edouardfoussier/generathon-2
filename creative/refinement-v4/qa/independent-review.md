## Follow-up: Nano Banana Pro reference treatments

Both new files probe as H.264, 1280 × 720, **5.041667 seconds**. I inspected six frames from each at **0.10, 1.00, 2.00, 3.00, 4.00 and 4.85 seconds**, plus one full-resolution still from each at 3 seconds. Thus these are twelve sampled Pro frames with two enlarged detail inspections, not a complete motion review. Evidence: `luna-pro-sheet.jpg`, `salsa-pro-sheet.jpg`, `review-luna-pro-detail.png` and `review-salsa-pro-detail.png`.

### Luna Pro

**Clear improvement over both FG01 and the prompt-only version for the stated face problem.** The forehead, nose, cheeks, jaw and neck now carry visible warm/cool paint planes. The highlight on the left side of the nose and the angled cheek/chin strokes read as deliberate painting rather than photographic skin pasted over the background. Texture treatment remains visible through the sampled turn and smile; no obvious return to smooth photographic shading appears in those six samples.

Luna remains recognizable through her dark tied-back curls, eyebrows, face outline and charcoal shirt. Stylization slightly idealizes/lengthens the features, so this is not a pixel-exact identity match. It is the strongest current compromise between a painted face and continuity with the existing film. The change from near-profile to a soft smile reads in the frame sequence, but motion smoothness, temporal texture crawling and in-between facial stability have not been fully auditioned.

### Salsa Pro

**Internally coherent, but a different visual direction.** Faces, hair, clothes, room and floor are consistently outlined and simplified into flat, largely cel-colored shapes. The large eyes, drawn mouths and clean facial shading move toward a 2D cartoon instead of the layered brushwork seen in Luna Pro. This avoids a pasted-photo face, but a five-second insertion would create a pronounced style jump from the basketball, shoe, wedding and family footage.

Rafa's black curls/terracotta shirt and Elena's curls/purple dress preserve character cues; the facial proportions are more freely redesigned than in the other salsa test. The hall also changes from the detailed windowed room to a simplified, symmetrical curtained stage, and the piano changes presentation. Samples show hands reaching, joining and a modest body shift. They do not establish a vigorous dance or improved montage energy.

### Updated recommendation

For the least disruptive experimental full cut: **Luna Pro + salsa prompt-only**, while retaining FG01 and all tests for the team to compare. Luna Pro addresses the actual paint-skin issue. Salsa prompt-only offers a moderate graphic adjustment without the much larger cartoon switch of Salsa Pro. If the team prefers Salsa Pro, use it as evidence for a deliberate broader restyling of the memory chapter; do not call a solitary insertion a seamless continuation of the approved visual style.

The phone scene still uses the smoother-face treatment and the different photograph noted above, so the film should remain labeled as an experimental comparison until those continuity choices are locked.
