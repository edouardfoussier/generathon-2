## New phone scene — generation brief

**7-second continuous setting with two or three editorial angles.** Luna, 15, the same low curly ponytail, plain charcoal oversized T-shirt and solid olive cargos, sits beside the open shoebox in D06. She holds one small aged photograph of young Rafa with a basketball and uses her phone to photograph it. Show the physical photo, phone and part of the attic together. She taps once, asks quietly or types, then lowers the phone toward the shoes with a thoughtful look. All skin, hair, hands, photograph and room share the same visible watercolor/gouache marks and pencil contours. No photographic face pasted onto a painted background, no smooth plastic skin. The screen is a modest dark/cream surface for later compositing, not a full-frame app demonstration. No invented readable generated text, no holograms, no duplicate phones, no giant shoes.

**Editorial treatment:** retain exact readable English text as a small screen insert or a restrained handwritten caption tied to the phone. ## Rhythm and music brief

- 0–15 s: sparse inquisitive motif with room for the two calls; understated pulse can start early.
- 15–26 s: dry tactile rhythm grows from shutter, lace and footfall. A short rest before the threshold.
- 26–45 s: lively original percussion, bass and warm melodic response; court energy blossoms into dance. Tempo is a creative choice, not inferred from the reference ad.
- 45–61 s: retain the pulse but reduce arrangement around newborn and father/daughter gesture.
- 60.2–72 s: Mom’s voice crosses time; music yields to two lines and reactions.
- 72–76 s: concise memorable resolve, leaving the endline unhurried.
