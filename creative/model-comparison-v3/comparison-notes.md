# Script v3 — first comparison notes

**Start with the Kling 3 Pro full cut as the working baseline.** Across the four sampled story passages, it gives the clearest continuous staging of the shoe handover and return to the attic, while preserving the established framing. Seedance 2.5 supplies the most useful alternative for more varied shot sizes, especially its gift sequence. Veo 3.1 makes the dance more active and gives warm reactions, but its selected cut needed more editorial correction.

This recommendation is provisional. It draws on each unit's QA notes and existing contact sheets for B07A, B09, B12 and B14A. It does **not** replace watching the assembled films at normal speed, and includes no judgement of soundtrack quality or dialogue synchronization.

The unit reports add two finishing considerations outside these four passages. Veo's first court-shoe clip invented an outer ankle badge and was successfully replaced. Its selected wedding range starts at 2 s to avoid an awkward opening leg lift. All versions still need a final check of photo identity, tiny shoe marks and readable editorial lettering.

**Recorded generation cost:** Kling 3 Pro: 3,856 credits; Seedance 2.5: 8,112 credits; Veo 3.1: 14,400 credits, including one correction. These totals describe this production run, not general model value.

This is a practical creative comparison, **not a controlled benchmark**. The story, references and final timing are shared, but the APIs condition on images differently; Seedance uses reference images while Kling and Veo use start frames. Source durations and selected trims differ, Veo returns eight-second sources, and only Veo received a correction. The common soundtrack and graphics belong to the edit, not to an individual model's demonstrated performance.
