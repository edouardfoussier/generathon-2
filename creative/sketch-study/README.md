# Illustrated style study

This study restages six existing story anchors in two visual languages without changing the selected cast, wardrobe, locations or black high-top product.

## Directions

- **Gouache on paper / Gouache sur papier:** translucent watercolor washes, opaque brush planes, visible warm paper and simplified peripheral scenery. Inspired by the team's `style_sketch` references.
- **Ink and hatching / Encre et hachures:** confident contour lines, directional pen hatching and restrained flat color. Inspired by the team's `style_sketch2` reference.

The reference screenshots guide visual technique only. Their interface icons, people, apparel and alternate sneaker designs are not requested as content.

## Six story moments

1. Luna in the attic — selected Luna and D06 setting.
2. Discovery of the inherited pair and basketball photograph.
3. Rafa on the Bronx basketball court, 1970.
4. Rafa meets Elena in the salsa hall, 1977; his black Chucks and her red Chucks.
5. Rafa offers the inherited shoe to his daughter, 1995.
6. Luna returns to the present and reconnects with her mother.

## Motion tests

Two eight-second Veo 3.1 clips were generated in parallel at 1080p / 24 fps from reviewed gouache frames: the court for body motion and the gift for gesture and emotion. These are style tests, not a complete animated edit. No subtitles, generated dialogue or score are requested.

## Production record

`plan.json` records prompts and reference roles. `jobs/` records submission IDs immediately. `manifest.json` identifies selected deliverables, actual charges and QA notes. Contact sheets and sampled motion frames are inspection aids.

The first simultaneous batch revealed that Arcads temporary style uploads were no longer reusable after their first use. Ten rejected calls were retried using the successful assets' permanent reference copies. The first gouache pass remained too close to photographic material, so a second pass prioritizes the paint technique and visible paper. These exploratory passes are preserved separately from the final selection.

## Results and cost

- 12 selected keyframes: six gouache and six ink, all 2752 × 1536.
- Two completed Veo 3.1 motion tests, each 8 seconds at 1920 × 1080 / 24 fps.
- 18 images charged at 16 credits (including the six earlier gouache studies): 288 credits.
- Two videos charged at 800 credits: 1,600 credits.
- Total: **1,888 credits**. Balance after: **14,462**.

Both videos decode fully and were visually sampled at one-second intervals. The gift scene maintains the illustrated medium particularly well and makes the family connection readable. The court scene establishes movement, though its moving character becomes slightly smoother than the initial drawing. The final edit should use a separate net insert if it needs an unequivocally scored basket. Product badge and repair details remain exploratory and need continuity cleanup before final branding. Native generated audio has not received a full listening review.

Potential direction: keep the present realistic, then let the inherited shoes carry Luna into painted memories. The shift of medium would have a narrative role, and the warm paper suggests preserved photographs and family traces. Test one present-to-painted match cut before regenerating a whole film.
